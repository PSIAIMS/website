Safety

Context

ABC-001 | Data cut: 01 October 2026 | Fictional study | Synthetic data | Demonstration only

TEAE = onset on/after first dose, through the cut, in the safety population. Percentages use the 180 first-dosed patients. Seriousness and severity are independent attributes. These simulated counts do not support treatment comparisons or clinical recommendations.

22 completed and 8 discontinued patients at this cut. AE discontinuations are a subset of all discontinued patients. A patient may have multiple events and contribute to multiple AE terms; term percentages need not sum to 100. Pre-dose events are excluded from TEAE. The 15 October cut is a simulated future snapshot for this demonstration.
